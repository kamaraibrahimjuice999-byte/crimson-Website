About Us

Building Sierra Leone's Future

Registered in Sierra Leone, CRIMSON TRADING COMPANY LIMITED is a multi-sector powerhouse driving development through media, tourism, agriculture, trade, and technology.

Our Vision

To be Sierra Leone's most trusted and innovative conglomerate — catalysing economic growth, empowering communities, and setting new standards across every sector we serve.

Our Mission

To deliver world-class multi-media production, tourism, agriculture, procurement, ICT, and sales promotion services that drive measurable impact for Sierra Leone and beyond.

Our Promise

Every engagement is a partnership. We combine local expertise with global standards to deliver excellence, integrity, and sustainable value for our clients and communities.

Our Journey

2009

Founded in Sierra Leone

CRIMSON TRADING COMPANY LIMITED established with a vision to transform Sierra Leone's business landscape.

2013

Media & Communications Launch

Entered multi-media production and TV broadcasting, becoming a leading voice in Sierra Leone's media space.

2016

Diversification

Expanded into tourism, agriculture, and fisheries — creating jobs and driving economic growth across the regions.

2020

Procurement & ICT Expansion

Launched procurement, office equipment supply, and ICT services — supporting government and private sector digital transformation.

2025

National Leader

7+ integrated business sectors, 300+ projects delivered, and a commitment to driving Sierra Leone's sustainable development.
